# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Primary users are merchants and buyers moving through a live commerce workflow in a mobile-first PWA context. Merchants set up a store profile and capture product inventory quickly with camera + voice to create a sharable storefront. Buyers browse active nearby merchants, compare products, and complete a quick handoff to payment.

## Product Purpose
Paytm Vision provides a practical, low-friction onboarding and storefront workflow for small merchants while exposing a clear buyer flow. The experience should keep users in one task: pick your role, capture or browse inventory, and move to an intentional next step without switching pages unnecessarily.

## Brand Personality
Precision-first, dependable, practical.

## Anti-references
Avoid high-slat glossy fintech/CRM aesthetics, heavy decorative gradients, excessive motion, and button/icon styles that do not behave consistently across screens. Avoid marketing-first hero layouts on core product pages.

## Design Principles
- Keep controls predictable: the same visual language for primary actions, secondary actions, and destructive states across all surfaces.
- Show what to do next in every state, including empty, loading, and completed states.
- Make errors understandable with one clear next step.
- Keep text readable on mobile and desktop first, with short scannable copy and clear hierarchy.
- Treat camera/voice inventory capture as a core workflow and reduce unnecessary friction there.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.1 AA is the default target.

Focus indicators, keyboard navigation, and button semantics must remain visible and consistent.
Contrast should remain usable in both bright and low-light environments.

The voice and inventory workflow should have clear fallback affordances when camera or speech APIs are unavailable.
